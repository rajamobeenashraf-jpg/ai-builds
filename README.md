# AI builds

I'm Mobeen Ashraf. I come from a business background, not a software one, and I run an AI-generated video channel where I already use AI tools every day to plan, produce, and publish. This repository is where I learn to build the systems behind that kind of work, in public, one week at a time.

## What you'll find here

Over the next six months this repository will fill up with working builds, each in its own folder with a short write-up of the problem, the build, and the result:

- Automations for real business tasks: scheduling, lead intake, comment triage, reporting, document extraction.
- AI systems that answer questions over a company's own documents, with measured accuracy.
- Agents that plan, use tools, check their own work, and run unattended.
- Connectors and setups that let an AI assistant work like a trained member of a specific business.
- Case studies from the above, written for business owners, not engineers.

Every build starts in my own business, gets tested until it runs without me, and only then becomes something I offer to clients.

## Why

Most small and medium businesses know AI could save them time but have no one to build it for them. I want to be the person who can sit with an owner, understand the actual problem, build the system that solves it, and explain it in plain language. That takes a business head and enough technical skill to deliver. I have the first; this repository is me building the second.

## Following along

I post what I build, what broke, and what I learned as I go. If you run a business and see something here that would help you, get in touch.
